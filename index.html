About me

Hey, I’m Manvendra Singh, a video editor, photographer, and videographer who loves capturing moments and turning them into stories.

For me, creating videos is not just about editing clips or capturing beautiful shots. It’s about showing a feeling, sharing a story, and making people see things from a different perspective.

Over the past 2 years, I’ve been exploring the world of video editing, photography, and filmmaking, working on different projects and learning something new with every experience.

I love experimenting with new ideas, playing with visuals, and finding creative ways to tell stories. Whether it’s a cinematic video, a travel film, an event, or content for a brand, I always try to put my heart into what I create.

I’m still learning, growing, and figuring things out, but that’s what makes this journey exciting.

This is where I share my work, my ideas, and my journey of becoming a better storyteller, one project at a time.
